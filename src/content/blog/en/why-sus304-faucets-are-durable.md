In high-end hotel construction and large-scale residential development, choosing sanitary fittings is never just about aesthetics — it is about durability, health and safety, and long-term total cost of ownership.

### 1. The Truth About Materials: SUS304 vs. Cheap Alloys

SDN Faucet's SUS304 stainless steel faucets offer natural corrosion and oxidation resistance. Compared with zinc alloys whose electroplated coating may peel over time, SUS304 maintains its brushed texture and structural integrity in humid environments for years.

### 2. SDN's Three Pillars of Quality Inspection

- **Air & Water Pressure Testing**: Every faucet is 100% tested at pressures exceeding 1.6MPa, guaranteeing zero leakage before it leaves the factory.
- **Salt Spray Testing**: Our products undergo 24–48 hours of acidic salt spray testing, ensuring the surface will not fade in coastal high-salinity environments.
- **Lifecycle Cycling Test**: Ceramic cartridges pass 500,000 on/off cycle tests, ensuring more than 15 years of daily-use service life.
